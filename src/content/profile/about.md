I am a first-year Master’s student in **Data Science** at University of Helsinki, and I expect to graduate in Summer 2028. Previously, I received my B.Eng. in Cyberspace Security from Wuhan University.

My research interests lie in **Trustworthy AI**, with a particular focus on **LLMs** and **Federated Learning**. I am interested in developing reliable, robust, and secure intelligent systems, as well as understanding and mitigating the security and privacy challenges that arise in real-world AI systems.

I feel fortunate to be supervised by [Prof. Hao Wang](https://intellisys.haow.us/haowang/) at Stevens Institute of Technology. Previously, I worked under the supervision of [Prof. Zeyan Liu](https://liuzey.com) at the University of Louisville.
